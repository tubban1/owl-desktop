# AI content and originality review — v1.0.0

**Scope:** Reusable Agent knowledge for social publishing. No execution permissions.

- Determine whether the source includes generated or substantially altered imagery, audio, or video. Follow the current destination platform's AI-labeling controls and rules; do not assume the same form across platforms.
- Copyright ownership, license permission, factual originality and platform "original creation" declarations are different concepts. Verify the actual content provenance and relevant platform conditions before making an affirmative originality declaration.
- A user's instruction to publish is not evidence that third-party materials are owned, nor that an AI label was completed.
- If the AI/content-rights status is unknown or the appropriate declaration control is missing, do not falsely report compliance; block the submission or request targeted review.
- After filling any declaration, inspect the page again to confirm its state. Record the evidence and platform-specific rule/version where available.
